We work one-on-one with you from start to finish. Here is what the process looks like.

<ol class="steps">
  <li class="step">
    <div class="step-num"><span>1</span></div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <h3>Reach out</h3>
      <p>Send us an email describing what you are working on. </p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="step">
    <div class="step-num"><span>2</span></div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <h3>Initial consultation</h3>
      <p>We hop on a call to talk through your data, your goals, and your constraints, and how we can solve your issues.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="step">
    <div class="step-num"><span>3</span></div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <h3>Proposal</h3>
      <p>We send you a written proposal covering scope, approach, timeline, and cost. We work on both fixed-scope projects and ongoing retainers.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="step">
    <div class="step-num"><span>4</span></div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <h3>Signed contract</h3>
      <p>Once we agree on the proposal, we sign a contract. Your data is yours: we do not retain, share, or use it for anything beyond the work we agreed to.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="step">
    <div class="step-num"><span>5</span></div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <h3>Execution</h3>
      <p>We get to work, check in regularly, and see the project to completion. You have direct access to the work products throughout.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ol>

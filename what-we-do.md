Twin Kites LLC turns data into actionable insights, with a focus on solutions that are private, scalable, and secure. We specialize in data analytics and engineering, including fully private generative AI (LLM) systems, large-scale database design and query optimization, statistical modeling, and interactive dashboards and visualizations.

We have worked extensively with many institutions, where data sensitivity and on-premise requirements are often non-negotiable. If your organization needs a solution that keeps data under your control (not sent to a third-party API, not stored off-site), that is exactly the kind of work we do.

### Why a kite?

The white kite's habit of hovering to gain a clear view before diving mirrors our approach to data work: understanding the problem carefully, thinking through its implications, and then executing precisely. To hover, the kite uses both its wings and tail, reflecting our blend of technical expertise ("wing") and strategic thinking ("tail"). The bird's sleek, minimalist nature also fits; we focus on clean, intuitive solutions and clearly documented code.

### Why twin kites?

The dual kites are, first, a tribute to family, to my brother and the connection that grounds this work. They also reflect something true about hard data problems: the best answers usually come from holding two things at once, whether that is speed and accuracy, openness and privacy, or complexity and clarity.
